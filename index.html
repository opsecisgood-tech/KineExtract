Tracking quality Careful and Exhaustive are several times slower on purpose. Two independent passes must agree; if they don't, tie-breaker passes run, then suspicious frames are re-measured after the sweep. Measures how the whole picture jumps between frames, then looks for the person where they should be after that jump. The person detector runs every frame while the camera is moving fast. The lasso and focus point never move on their own. Turn off for a tripod shot to save a little time.
Human tracker engine The advanced engine runs fully in your browser (WebGPU, falls back to CPU). Models download once, about 350 MB, then stay cached. The model/confidence boxes below only apply to MediaPipe.

Background rebuild (AI)
Off. Tick the box and the background is rebuilt right after every Analyze, and added to the Alight export. The AI cuts the tracked person out of every Nth frame. What they were standing in front of is filled in from other moments where it was visible, so you get a clean background without them. Static uses a wide time window (tripod shots). Moving uses a short window and follows the camera movement measured by the camera-shake compensation in the Scan settings above, so keep that ticked. Detail sets how big the captured picture is (Photo is the closest to the real image but slow). The Low / Mid / High / Ultra / Photo setting for how many parts the background has lives in the Alight Motion export section (Export tab).
Who to track Use Pick subject above the video on the first frame: everyone found is numbered, click one. Their clothing colours are memorised and tracking stays on them even when people overlap. Drag the circle to nudge it. With no pick, the largest, clearest person is used.
Focus lasso Go to the first frame, press Lasso above the video, then drag a loop around the spot to track. Tracking zooms in around the lasso center and prefers the person inside it. The lasso and the subject circle stay put until you drag their center handle somewhere else.
Body partsUntick a part to remove it from tracking, graphs and exports. Press Analyze again to re-track without it.
Camera shake Positions of every part, graphs, keyframes and the Alight export are corrected for camera movement, so the character stays in one spot instead of jumping with the camera. Movement slower than the cutoff is treated as a real pan and kept. Rotations and joint angles are never touched. Needs the compensation box in Scan ticked when you press Analyze.

Alight Motion export Builds a real Alight Motion project (.xml): one layer per body part with position, length and rotation keyframes, eased like your graphs. Time 0 = start of the clip, so drop your video under it at the same start. Before saving, two automatic checks look for parts that overlap where they should not, then you confirm the preview yourself.
Compatibility The Alight XML format is community-documented, not official. Import the calibration project (button below) and set these two to whatever behaves as the layer names say. Written into every shape as Alight Motion's own size property; the Scale values are computed to match it, so any value gives the same drawing. 100 is Alight Motion's default. Leave it alone unless the SIZE row in the calibration project does not reach almost edge to edge.

About KineExtract

Video to motion, in your browser.

What it does

KineExtract turns video into animation. It tracks a person through a clip, builds clean keyframes with editable easing, and exports the result as CSV, JSON, a reference sheet or a ready-to-open Alight Motion project.
