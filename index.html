Character recognition (AI) Cuts the character out on every frame (SlimSAM), follows it by shape and colour, then reads the pose from only that cut-out. About 50 MB of models download once, then stay cached.
Tracking quality Careful and Exhaustive are several times slower on purpose. Two independent passes must agree; if they don't, tie-breaker passes run, then suspicious frames are re-measured after the sweep.
Human tracker engine The advanced engine runs fully in your browser (WebGPU, falls back to CPU). Models download once, about 350 MB, then stay cached. The model/confidence boxes below only apply to MediaPipe.

Who to track Use Pick subject above the video on the first frame: everyone found is numbered, click one. Their clothing colours are memorised and tracking stays on them even when people overlap. Drag the circle to nudge it. With no pick, the largest, clearest person is used. Use Pick character above the video on the first frame, then click the character. Click more spots to add, Shift+click to remove, and use Larger / Smaller to change how much is included (just the outfit, the whole body, or the group).
Focus lasso Go to the first frame, press Lasso above the video, then drag a loop around the spot to track. Tracking zooms in around the lasso center and prefers the person inside it. The lasso and the subject circle stay put until you drag their center handle somewhere else.
Body partsUntick a part to remove it from tracking, graphs and exports. Press Analyze again to re-track without it.
